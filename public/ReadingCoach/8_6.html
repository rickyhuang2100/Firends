🌏 中文翻譯

逐句對照
English:
Borobudur is one of the most remarkable Buddhist monuments in the world.		 中文:
婆羅浮屠是世界上最非凡的佛教紀念碑之一。
English:
This temple is made from volcanic rock, and contains 504 statues of Buddha and thousands of other sculptures that share the Buddha’s teachings, life, and wisdom.		 中文:
這座寺廟由火山岩建成，包含504尊佛像和數千件其他雕塑，分享著佛陀的教誨、生平和智慧。
English:
Borobudur is located in the Kedu valley of central Java, a large Indonesian island.		 中文:
婆羅浮屠位於印尼大島爪哇中部的克杜谷地。
English:
It lies between two volcanoes and two rivers.		 中文:
它位於兩座火山和兩條河流之間。
English:
The ancient Javanese considered the Kedu Valley to be one of the most sacred sites in Java because of its fertile land and high agricultural production.		 中文:
古代爪哇人認為克杜谷地是爪哇最神聖的地點之一，因為它有肥沃的土地和很高的農業產量。
English:
Historians believe that Borobudur was completed around the year 800 CE.		 中文:
歷史學家認為婆羅浮屠大約在西元800年完工。
English:
Several centuries earlier, Hindu and Buddhist merchants from India had settled in the region.		 中文:
幾個世紀前，來自印度的印度教和佛教商人已經在該地區定居。
English:
The Javanese incorporated parts of the culture and religions of ancient India into their own.		 中文:
爪哇人將古印度文化和宗教的一部分融入了自己的文化和宗教中。
English:
Borobudur’s architecture is an example of this blending of cultures because it contains both ancient Indian and Javanese influences.		 中文:
婆羅浮屠的建築就是這種文化融合的例子，因為它包含了古印度和爪哇的影響。
English:
Buddhists among the Javanese population made pilgrimages and performed rituals at this holy site up until the 15th century.		 中文:
爪哇人口中的佛教徒直到15世紀都在這個聖地朝聖並舉行儀式。
English:
After the 15th century, the temple was abandoned.		 中文:
15世紀之後，這座寺廟被廢棄了。
English:
Some historians believe Borobudur was abandoned because many Javanese converted to Islam during this time.		 中文:
一些歷史學家認為婆羅浮屠被廢棄是因為這段期間許多爪哇人皈依了伊斯蘭教。
English:
Others argue that volcanic eruptions near Borobudur may have stopped people from visiting this site.		 中文:
其他人則認為，婆羅浮屠附近的火山爆發可能阻止了人們參觀這個地點。
English:
However, much mystery remains about why this site was abandoned.		 中文:
然而，關於這個地點為何被廢棄，仍然存在許多謎團。
English:
As time went by, the temple was covered in volcanic ash and vegetation.		 中文:
隨著時間流逝，這座寺廟被火山灰和植被所覆蓋。
English:
It was no longer visible.		 中文:
它不再可見。
English:
Although local people knew of this temple from legends, the site was no longer used.		 中文:
雖然當地人從傳說中知道這座寺廟，但這個地點已不再被使用。
English:
In 1814, an English lieutenant governor heard these legends from the islanders.		 中文:
1814年，一位英國副總督從島民那裡聽說了這些傳說。
English:
He traveled to the place the local people had talked about and uncovered the temple from under layers of volcanic ash.		 中文:
他前往當地人談論的那個地方，並從層層火山灰下挖掘出這座寺廟。
English:
Archaeologists worked with the Indonesian government to restore the temple from 1907 to 1911 and again in the 1970s.		 中文:
考古學家與印尼政府合作，在1907年到1911年之間，以及在1970年代再次修復了這座寺廟。
English:
Although the full history of this temple remains unclear, historians have uncovered certain facts about the structure’s symbolism.		 中文:
雖然這座寺廟的完整歷史仍然不清楚，但歷史學家已經揭露了關於其建築象徵意義的一些事實。
English:
The structure of Borobudur symbolizes the Buddhist path to enlightenment.		 中文:
婆羅浮屠的結構象徵著佛教的開悟之路。
English:
Borobudur is a stepped pyramid that has three main levels.		 中文:
婆羅浮屠是一座階梯式的金字塔，有三個主要樓層。
English:
These main levels represent the three spheres of the universe, according to Buddhist understanding.		 中文:
根據佛教的理解，這三個主要樓層代表了宇宙的三個界。
English:
They symbolize the spiritual journey through which individuals try to achieve “formlessness,” or full detachment from the physical world and earthly desires.		 中文:
它們象徵著一趟精神旅程，個人透過這趟旅程試圖達到「無色」，即完全超脫物質世界和世俗慾望。
English:
The temple’s first level is a square base that represents kamadhatu.		 中文:
寺廟的第一層是一個方形的基座，代表「欲界 (kamadhatu)」。
English:
This is the Sphere of Desire, or the lowest sphere of the Buddhist universe.		 中文:
這就是欲界，或佛教宇宙中最低的界。
English:
On this first level, there are sculptures of earthly desires and pleasures.		 中文:
在這第一層，有描繪世俗慾望和歡樂的雕塑。
English:
The second level represents rupadhatu, the Sphere of Forms.		 中文:
第二層代表「色界 (rupadhatu)」。
English:
It consists of five square terraces.		 中文:
它由五個方形露台組成。
English:
On this level, sculptures of events from the Buddha’s life line the passageways.		 中文:
在這一層，描繪佛陀生平事件的雕塑排列在通道兩側。
English:
The highest level contains three circular terraces.		 中文:
最高層包含三個圓形露台。
English:
It represents arupadhatu, the Sphere of Formlessness.		 中文:
它代表「無色界 (arupadhatu)」。
English:
This is the highest sphere of existence in Buddhist ideology and the last step on the path to enlightenment.		 中文:
這是佛教意識形態中存在的最高境界，也是通往開悟之路的最後一步。
English:
On the highest level of the temple, there are dome-shaped shrines to the Buddha called stupas.		 中文:
在寺廟的最高層，有圓頂狀的佛龕，稱為「佛塔 (stupas)」。
English:
These stupas symbolize the enlightened mind, a mind free of earthly desires and attachments.		 中文:
這些佛塔象徵著開悟的心靈，一個擺脫了世俗慾望和執著的心靈。
English:
The temple’s three levels are connected by open-air passageways.		 中文:
寺廟的三個樓層由露天通道連接。
English:
The passageways circle around a central point.		 中文:
通道圍繞著一個中心點。
English:
Buddhists who visit this site travel clockwise through these passageways until they reach the top level.		 中文:
參觀這個地點的佛教徒會順時針穿過這些通道，直到他們到達頂層。
English:
In Buddhist philosophy, individuals work towards enlightenment by developing concentration, morality, and wisdom.		 中文:
在佛教哲學中，個人透過培養定力、道德和智慧來努力達到開悟。
English:
Over the centuries, walking the paths of Borobudur has allowed Buddhists to meditate on their spiritual journey.		 中文:
幾個世紀以來，走在婆羅浮屠的道路上，讓佛教徒能夠反思他們的精神旅程。
English:
Historians have also learned about the history of the Javanese people through this architectural marvel.		 中文:
歷史學家也透過這座建築奇蹟，了解了爪哇人民的歷史。

✏️ 文法重點解析

convert to (皈依；轉變為)

表示從一種信仰、觀點或狀態轉變為另一種。

Many Javanese converted to Islam during this time.
He converted to Buddhism.
work towards (努力達成)

表示為了實現某個目標或結果而努力。

Individuals work towards enlightenment by developing concentration.
We are working towards a better future.

💬 重要句型分析

as time goes by (隨著時間流逝)

表示時間的推移。在過去式中為 as time went by。

As time went by, the temple was covered in volcanic ash.
free of (免於...的；沒有...的)

表示不受某事物影響或牽絆。

These stupas symbolize a mind free of earthly desires.

🎯 閱讀理解

理解測驗
  1. 婆羅浮屠寺廟是由什麼材料建成的？
    • A) 大理石 (Marble)
    • B) 火山岩 (Volcanic rock)
    • C) 木頭 (Wood)
    • D) 石灰岩 (Limestone)

    正確答案：B

  2. 根據佛教的理解，婆羅浮屠的三個主要樓層代表什麼？
    • A) 人類生命的三個階段
    • B) 宇宙的三個界 (The three spheres of the universe)
    • C) 爪哇最神聖的三條河流
    • D) 古印度的三個主要影響

    正確答案：B

  3. 為什麼歷史學家認為婆羅浮屠在15世紀後被廢棄了？
    • A) 寺廟被地震摧毀了。
    • B) 當地人對佛教失去了興趣。
    • C) 許多爪哇人皈依了伊斯蘭教或發生了火山爆發。
    • D) 建築正在沉入肥沃的土地中。

    正確答案：C
