🌏 中文翻譯

乘麗·乞沙姆(Shirley Chisholm)

你長大後想做什麼？也許你想當醫生。也許你想當藝術家。也許你想幫忙制定法律，就像乘麗·乞沙姆(Shirley Chisholm)一樣。Shirley Chisholm 想要進入國會。國會是美國政府的一部分。國會裡的人為每個人制定法律。Shirley 想要制定幫助人們的法律。人們投票讓 Shirley 進入國會。她成為第一位進入國會的黑人女性。許多人敬佩 Shirley。她很聰明又勇敢。她關心人們。

逐句對照
English:
What do you want to be when you grow up? 		中文:
你長大後想做什麼？
Maybe you want to become a doctor. 也許你想當醫生。
Maybe you want to become an artist. 也許你想當藝術家。
Maybe you want to help make laws, like Shirley Chisholm. 也許你想幫忙制定法律，就像 Shirley Chisholm 一樣。
Shirley Chisholm wanted to be in Congress. Shirley Chisholm 想要進入國會。
Congress is part of the United States government. 國會是美國政府的一部分。
People in Congress make laws for everyone to follow. 國會裡的人為每個人制定法律。
Shirley wanted to make laws that help people. Shirley 想要制定幫助人們的法律。
People voted for Shirley to be in Congress. 人們投票讓 Shirley 進入國會。
She became the first black woman to be in Congress. 她成為第一位進入國會的黑人女性。
Many people look up to Shirley. 許多人敬佩 Shirley。
She was smart and brave. 她很聰明又勇敢。
She cared about people. 她關心人們。

✏️ 文法重點解析

1. "Maybe you want to + 動詞" 也許你想……
Maybe you want to become a doctor.
Maybe you want to become an artist.
Maybe you want to help make laws.
2. "wanted to + 動詞" 想要做某事
Shirley Chisholm wanted to be in Congress.
3. "the first + 名詞 + to + 動詞"
She became the first black woman to be in Congress.

💬 重要句型分析

句型: What do you want to be?
What do you want to be when you grow up?
句型: make laws for... to follow
People in Congress make laws for everyone to follow.
句型: look up to 敬佩
Many people look up to Shirley.

🎯 閱讀理解技巧

人物介紹
  • Shirley Chisholm 是第一位進入美國國會的黑人女性
  • 她聰明、勇敢，關心人民
  • 她製定法律來幫助人們
  • 很多人都敬佩她
理解測驗
  1. What did Shirley Chisholm want to do? (Shirley 想做什麼？)
  2. What is Congress? (國會是什麼？)
  3. Why is Shirley special? (為什麼 Shirley 很特別？)
  4. What kind of person was Shirley? (Shirley 是什麼樣的人？)
