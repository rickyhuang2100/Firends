📜 英文原文文本

Brazil's flag symbolizes the government of the people. The banner that stretches across the middle of the sphere reads: "Ordem e Progresso." This means "Order and Progress" in the language of Portuguese. Portuguese is the official language of Brazil.

The flag also symbolizes the lush landscape of Brazil. Brazil also has beautiful natural resources. The blue sphere around the stars in the flag resembles the bright blue sky over Brazil. Only in tropical climates is the sky so blue. The yellow in the flag in the shape of a diamond represents gold and diamonds. The early wealth of Brazil came from gold and diamond mining. The green in the flag symbolizes the country's deep forests and fertile fields. Brazil also has profited from its sugar plantations, coffee plantations, and rubber-making businesses. These natural resources helped determine the social and economic character of Brazil. In the colonial era, many Portuguese adventurers came to Brazil. The Portuguese brought slaves from West Africa to work on sugar plantations. Today, Brazil's population is a mixture of these groups – West Africans, Portuguese, and also Native Americans.
