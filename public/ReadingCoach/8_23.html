逐句對照
English:
By this time, many people had been trying to invent an airplane that worked.		 中文:
到了這個時候，許多人一直試圖發明一架能運作的飛機。
English:
Some airplanes were steam-powered; others had flapping wings, like birds.		 中文:
有些飛機是由蒸汽驅動的；其他則有像鳥一樣撲翼的翅膀。
English:
The German inventor Otto Lilienthal, also known as “The Glider King,” was appearing in all the papers, showing off his gliding machine, which he’d built based on his studies of storks.		 中文:
德國發明家奧托·李林塔爾（Otto Lilienthal），也被稱為「滑翔機之王」，出現在各大報紙上，展示他的滑翔機，這是他根據對鸛鳥的研究而建造的。
English:
Though his gliders had no motors, instead coasting purely on air, they could keep a pilot hanging in the air under the right conditions.		 中文:
雖然他的滑翔機沒有馬達，純粹依靠空氣滑行，但在適當的條件下，它們可以讓飛行員懸浮在空中。
English:
When Lilienthal made headlines again by plunging to his death in August of 1896 during a gliding accident, inventors knew they had to improve upon his designs, or else.		 中文:
當李林塔爾在1896年8月的一次滑翔事故中墜落身亡再次成為頭條新聞時，發明家們知道他們必須改進他的設計，否則（也會遭遇同樣的命運）。
English:
There was no Internet to research designs back in 1896, so the brothers had to write letters to the Smithsonian Institution (today the world’s largest museum and research complex) to request more information about early flight pioneers.		 中文:
在1896年，那時候沒有網路可以研究設計，所以這對兄弟不得不寫信給史密森尼學會（如今是世界上最大的博物館和研究綜合體），以索取更多關於早期飛行先驅的資訊。
English:
They pored over everything they could find, from Lilienthal’s designs to the diagrams of Leonardo da Vinci, who had sketched designs for both gliders and helicopters as early as the 1400s.		 中文:
他們仔細鑽研了他們能找到的一切，從李林塔爾的設計到李奧納多·達文西的圖紙，達文西早在1400年代就已經繪製了滑翔機和直升機的設計草圖。
English:
And like many inventors before them, they watched a lot of birds.		 中文:
和他們之前的許多發明家一樣，他們觀察了許多鳥類。
English:
One big difference between birds and early gliders, the brothers noticed, was that birds were able to “roll” right or left as the winds changed, and turned by shifting the angles of their wings.		 中文:
這對兄弟注意到，鳥類和早期滑翔機之間的一個很大區別是，當風向改變時，鳥類能夠向右或向左「側滾」，並透過改變翅膀的角度來轉向。
English:
Much like a person riding a bicycle, they thought birds could “bank” or lean into turns, while still remaining stable and upright.		 中文:
就像騎自行車的人一樣，他們認為鳥類可以在轉彎時「傾斜」或側傾，同時仍保持穩定和直立。
English:
By contrast, a glider pilot did not have the same level of control as a bird, leading him or her to topple over or crash, as Lilienthal did.		 中文:
相比之下，滑翔機飛行員沒有像鳥類那樣的控制水平，導致他們翻倒或墜毀，就像李林塔爾那樣。
English:
The Wright brothers loved experimenting with flight, but they certainly didn’t want to die trying, so from the beginning, they focused on bringing more control to the flying experience.		 中文:
萊特兄弟喜歡進行飛行實驗，但他們當然不想在嘗試中喪生，所以從一開始，他們就專注於為飛行體驗帶來更多控制。
English:
The brothers drew from their research of flying machines and utilized the design principles from the bicycle world.		 中文:
這對兄弟借鑒了他們對飛行機器的研究，並利用了自行車領域的設計原理。
English:
Once they figured out how to control the movement of a machine as well as their bird friends could control their bodies, the brothers thought they could easily find the right parts to add onto wings and motors to create a viable flying machine.		 中文:
一旦他們弄清楚如何像他們的鳥類朋友控制身體一樣控制機器的運動，兄弟倆認為他們可以很容易地找到合適的零件加到翅膀和馬達上，以創造一台可行的飛行機器。
English:
This approach was very different from the approaches of other would-be airplane inventors of the day.		 中文:
這種方法與當時其他想要發明飛機的發明家的方法截然不同。
English:
Other engineers were studying trains or ships, and thus, spent most of their time building stronger engines (as it was thought that a strong engine could propel a plane into the air more easily) or ship-like rudders to guide the airplanes along.		 中文:
其他工程師正在研究火車或船隻，因此，花費大部分時間建造更強大的引擎（因為人們認為強大的引擎可以更容易地將飛機推進空中）或類似船的方向舵來引導飛機前進。
English:
As it turned out, the Wright brothers had the winning approach.		 中文:
事實證明，萊特兄弟擁有致勝的方法。
English:
Once the brothers had decided to begin testing their experiments with gliders, they needed to find a better place to glide as Ohio wasn’t quite windy enough.		 中文:
一旦兄弟倆決定開始用滑翔機測試他們的實驗，他們需要找到一個更好的滑翔地點，因為俄亥俄州的風還不夠大。
English:
After writing to the U.S. Weather Bureau, analyzing the weather data for various areas and asking other inventors for advice, the Wright brothers decided that Kitty Hawk in North Carolina would be the best place to take advantage of sea breezes for gliding practice.		 中文:
在寫信給美國氣象局、分析各個地區的天氣數據並徵求其他發明家的建議後，萊特兄弟決定北卡羅來納州的基蒂霍克（Kitty Hawk）將是利用海風進行滑翔練習的最佳地點。
English:
From 1900 to 1903, they set up camp at Kitty Hawk, testing designs by making “glider kites” that could be flown without pilots for safety.		 中文:
從1900年到1903年，他們在基蒂霍克紮營，透過製作為了安全可以在沒有飛行員的情況下飛行的「滑翔風箏」來測試設計。
English:
Still comparing human innovations with birds in flight, the brothers changed each small part of their design.		 中文:
兄弟倆仍然將人類的創新與飛行中的鳥類進行比較，改變了他們設計的每一個小部分。
English:
Some had giant wings; some came without tails.		 中文:
有些有巨大的翅膀；有些沒有尾巴。
English:
Many were still crashing, but the brothers tried not to become frustrated.		 中文:
許多仍然在墜毀，但兄弟倆努力不讓自己感到沮喪。
English:
During one winter, they returned home to Ohio, where they tried different ways of testing that weren’t so expensive.		 中文:
在一個冬天，他們回到俄亥俄州的家，在那裡他們嘗試了不那麼昂貴的不同的測試方法。
English:
On some days, they could be seen pedaling bicycles equipped with wings down the streets and startling passersby.		 中文:
在某些日子裡，可以看到他們踩著裝有翅膀的自行車在街道上行駛，讓路人感到驚訝。
English:
On others, they'd create tiny airplane prototypes to test in the Ohio wind.		 中文:
在另一些日子裡，他們會製作微型的飛機原型，在俄亥俄州的風中進行測試。
English:
Over time, the brothers discovered many errors in the designs of the inventors that had come before them, including Lilienthal, and fixed them by using their own calculations.		 中文:
隨著時間的推移，兄弟倆發現了在他們之前的發明家（包括李林塔爾）設計中的許多錯誤，並使用他們自己的計算來修正這些錯誤。
English:
The biggest difference between riding a bicycle and flying a plane, they realized, was that machines on the ground only had to move forward or backward in order to get going.		 中文:
他們意識到，騎自行車和開飛機之間最大的區別在於，地面上的機器只需要向前或向後移動就可以運作。
English:
An airplane had to be controlled in three dimensions: it could point up or down, curve from side to side, and roll upside-down.		 中文:
飛機必須在三個維度上進行控制：它可以指向上或下，從一側彎向另一側，並上下顛倒地側滾。
English:
So they knew their final design had to have elements that controlled movement in each of these three ways to keep them from crashing.		 中文:
所以他們知道，他們的最終設計必須包含以這三種方式中的每一種來控制運動的元素，以防止它們墜毀。
English:
The resulting design was called “three-axis control,” and it is still used today.		 中文:
由此產生的設計被稱為「三軸控制」，至今仍在使用。
English:
The Wright brothers designed three-axis control to help the pilot guide the plane in all three dimensions.		 中文:
萊特兄弟設計了三軸控制來幫助飛行員在所有三個維度上引導飛機。
English:
They created one innovation called “wing-warping” which allows the pilot to bend (or “warp”) the wings and help to turn the plane like a bird might do with its wings.		 中文:
他們創造了一項名為「機翼扭曲 (wing-warping)」的創新技術，允許飛行員彎曲（或「扭曲」）機翼，並幫助像鳥類用翅膀那樣轉動飛機。
English:
They created another innovation called the “forward elevator” which helps the pilot to turn the plane upwards, toward the sky, or downward, toward the ground.		 中文:
他們創造了另一項名為「前升降舵 (forward elevator)」的創新技術，幫助飛行員將飛機轉向上、朝向天空，或轉向下、朝向地面。
English:
And finally, they figured out a way to use that ship rudder after all: it could be used during turns to keep the plane from flipping over, as it had done in earlier gliding experiments.		 中文:
最後，他們終於想出了一種使用那個船舵的方法：它可以在轉彎時使用，以防止飛機翻覆，就像它在早期的滑翔實驗中發生的那樣。
English:
By 1902, the Wright brothers were piloting hundreds of well-controlled glider flights (between September and October of 1902, they made between 700 and 1,000 glides).		 中文:
到了1902年，萊特兄弟已經駕駛了數百次控制良好的滑翔機飛行（在1902年9月至10月期間，他們進行了700至1000次滑翔）。
English:
They finally felt confident enough to patent their “three-axis control” system.		 中文:
他們終於感到足夠自信，為他們的「三軸控制」系統申請專利。
English:
And they were ready to add engines to the equation.		 中文:
他們準備將引擎加入方程式中（準備加上引擎了）。
English:
After searching around for the right motor for weeks, the brothers asked their bike-shop buddy, Charlie Taylor, to try his hand at creating a motor for an airplane.		 中文:
在四處尋找合適的馬達幾個星期後，兄弟倆請他們的自行車店夥伴查理·泰勒（Charlie Taylor）嘗試為飛機製造一個馬達。
English:
In six weeks, he had it ready.		 中文:
六個星期內，他就準備好了。
English:
Just one year after they created their three-axis system, the brothers made their first motorized flight, and by doing so, made history.		 中文:
在他們發明三軸系統的一年後，兄弟倆進行了他們的第一次機動飛行，並藉此創造了歷史。

✏️ 文法重點解析

or else (否則；要不然)

用來表示如果不做某事，就會發生不好的結果。

Inventors knew they had to improve upon his designs, or else (they would crash).
Hurry up, or else you will miss the train.
try one's hand at (嘗試做...)

表示第一次或試著去做某件事，看自己是否有能力完成。

The brothers asked Charlie to try his hand at creating a motor.
I decided to try my hand at baking a cake.

💬 重要句型分析

as + 形容詞/副詞 + as... (和...一樣...)

用來比較兩個人或事物，表示兩者在某方面程度相同。

Once they figured out how to control the movement of a machine as well as their bird friends could control their bodies...

理解測驗
  1. 奧托·李林塔爾（Otto Lilienthal）的滑翔機是根據什麼設計的？
    • A) 火車。
    • B) 船隻。
    • C) 鸛鳥。
    • D) 自行車。

    正確答案：C

  2. 萊特兄弟發明飛行機器的方法與其他發明家有何不同？
    • A) 他們專注於先製造一個更強大的引擎。
    • B) 他們研究船隻來製造飛機。
    • C) 他們專注於為飛行體驗帶來更多控制，使用了自行車的設計原理。
    • D) 他們根本沒有研究鳥類。

    正確答案：C

  3. 為什麼萊特兄弟選擇北卡羅來納州的基蒂霍克（Kitty Hawk）進行測試？
    • A) 因為它離他們在俄亥俄州的家很近。
    • B) 因為他們想靠近史密森尼學會。
    • C) 因為俄亥俄州的風對他們的原型機來說太大了。
    • D) 因為這是一個利用海風進行滑翔練習的好地方。

    正確答案：D
