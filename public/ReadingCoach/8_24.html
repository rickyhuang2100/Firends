逐句對照
English:
Imagine a life directly defined by roughly a third of a year of rain and flooding.		 中文:
想像一種生活，其直接由大約一年中三分之一時間的降雨和洪水所決定。
English:
The people of ancient Mesopotamia, Egypt, and the Kingdom of Kush all lived that life: their economies, power, and simple survival depended on the seasons of the rivers that ran through each empire.		 中文:
古代美索不達米亞、埃及和庫施王國的人民都過著這樣的生活：他們的經濟、權力，以及單純的生存都依賴於流經每個帝國的河流的季節。
English:
Ancient Egypt and Mesopotamia made up parts of the area known as the Fertile Crescent, which experienced rain every year for about 100 days, beginning in the late spring or early summer.		 中文:
古埃及和美索不達米亞構成了被稱為「肥沃月彎 (Fertile Crescent)」地區的一部分，該地區每年經歷約100天的降雨，從晚春或初夏開始。
English:
For ancient Egypt, this caused the Nile River to flood, which saturated the normally arid land around it with water and nutrient-rich, river-born soil called silt.		 中文:
對古埃及來說，這導致了尼羅河氾濫，這使周圍原本乾旱的土地充滿了水分，以及富含營養、由河流帶來的被稱為淤泥 (silt) 的土壤。
English:
Ancient Egyptians are now revered as the masters of desert agriculture, for their irrigation technology allowed them to cultivate crops during the dry months, from a fall-season sowing to springtime harvest. 		中文:
古埃及人現在被尊崇為沙漠農業的大師，因為他們的灌溉技術使他們能夠在乾旱的月份裡種植農作物，從秋季播種到春季收穫。
English:
Their expertise started with simply monitoring weather patterns and gauging the rise and fall of the Nile’s water levels, practices by which the nation’s people were able to plan their planting and harvest seasons accordingly.		 中文:
他們的專業知識始於簡單地監測天氣模式並測量尼羅河水位的漲落，透過這些做法，該國人民能夠相應地計劃他們的種植和收穫季節。
English:
Some special inventions revolving around the rainy season included the system of dikes and canals built to contain and direct the floodwaters of the Nile.		 中文:
一些圍繞著雨季的特殊發明包括為了容納和引導尼羅河洪水而建造的堤防和運河系統。
English:
By diverting water, ancient Egyptians were able to keep it from washing onto un-farmable desert terrain, where it would essentially be wasted.		 中文:
透過引水，古埃及人能夠防止水沖刷到無法耕種的沙漠地形上，在那裡水基本上會被浪費掉。
English:
Instead, the ability to move water to the crops that needed it allowed for a productive growing season and higher crop yield. 		中文:
相反地，將水引到需要它的農作物上的能力，帶來了多產的生長季節和更高的農作物產量。
English:
Ancient Egyptians grew a variety of crops, and they were able to build cities around the abundance.		 中文:
古埃及人種植各種農作物，並且他們能夠圍繞著這種豐富的物產建立城市。
English:
They traded grains (and in surplus years, could store excess in granaries as well), made linen from flax, and sustained themselves on garden crops grown in smaller plots, often part of personal households. 		中文:
他們交易穀物（在豐收的年份，也能將多餘的存儲在穀倉中），用亞麻製作亞麻布，並靠在通常屬於私人家庭一部分的較小地塊上種植的園藝作物來維持生活。
English:
Cities thrived along the Nile, in large part because of the proximity to the obvious transportation, which facilitated trade. 		中文:
城市沿著尼羅河繁榮發展，在很大程度上是因為靠近這條顯而易見的交通要道，這促進了貿易。
English:
As the fruits of farm labor were traded, city merchants grew wealthier, and the metropolis thrived.		 中文:
隨著農作勞動的成果被交易，城市商人變得更加富有，大都會也隨之繁榮。
English:
The same sort of growth was characteristic of the nearby kingdom of Kush, also known as Nubia.		 中文:
同樣的成長也是附近庫施王國（又稱努比亞）的特徵。
English:
Kush was situated just south of ancient Egypt, in what is now southern Egypt and northern Sudan.		 中文:
庫施位於古埃及的正南方，即現在的埃及南部和蘇丹北部。
English:
Kush’s two capitals, first Napata and then Meroe, were each situated along the Nile.		 中文:
庫施的兩個首都，首先是納帕塔（Napata），然後是麥羅埃（Meroe），都位於尼羅河畔。
English:
The Kushites employed many of the same irrigation techniques as the ancient Egyptians did, taking advantage of the rainy season to keep the land fertile.		 中文:
庫施人採用了許多與古埃及人相同的灌溉技術，利用雨季來保持土地肥沃。
English:
Using dams and cataracts, the Kushites directed the flow of water to grow wheat, barley, lentils, peas, and even dates and mangoes.		 中文:
利用水壩和瀑布（急流），庫施人引導水流來種植小麥、大麥、扁豆、豌豆，甚至棗子和芒果。
English:
Waterwheels known as “saqia” allowed for even further movement of water to higher ground.		 中文:
被稱為「沙基亞 (saqia)」的水車，讓水能進一步輸送到更高的地方。
English:
Midway through the golden years of Kush (called, too, the “Land of Gold” for its gold trade), the arrival of iron changed farming forever.		 中文:
在庫施（因其黃金貿易也被稱為「黃金之地」）的黃金時代中期，鐵的到來永遠改變了農業。
English:
An invasion by the Assyrians cut short the influence of Kush in Egypt, but while they lost power over their Northern neighbors, the Kushites gained knowledge of ironwork.		 中文:
亞述人的入侵縮短了庫施在埃及的影響力，但雖然他們失去了對北方鄰國的控制權，庫施人卻獲得了鐵器製造的知識。
English:
The deserts east of the Nile near the city of Meroe were rich in iron ore, and as Kush began to make tools such as hoes and plows, crop yields increased.		 中文:
麥羅埃城附近尼羅河以東的沙漠富含鐵礦，隨著庫施開始製造鋤頭和犁等工具，農作物產量增加了。
English:
Again, with the proximity of the harvest to the river—the main means of trade and travel—cities like Napata and Meroe grew and enjoyed prosperity with the agriculture-driven commerce.		 中文:
同樣，由於收穫地靠近河流——貿易和旅行的主要手段——像納帕塔和麥羅埃這樣的城市在農業驅動的商業推動下成長並享有繁榮。
English:
Located across the Red Sea and the Syrian Desert from Kush, there was the famous region of the Fertile Crescent called Mesopotamia.		 中文:
在庫施隔著紅海和敘利亞沙漠的地方，有著被稱為美索不達米亞的著名肥沃月彎地區。
English:
Framed by the Tigris and Euphrates rivers, it occupied what is now Iraq.		 中文:
它以底格里斯河和幼發拉底河為界，佔據了現在的伊拉克。
English:
The area experienced the same cyclical flooding periods that ancient Egypt and Kush did, and thus had to learn to adapt.		 中文:
該地區經歷了與古埃及和庫施相同的週期性洪水時期，因此必須學會適應。
English:
The difference is, this “land between rivers” (the meaning of the origin of the name “Mesopotamia”) had the flooding of not just one waterway but an entire flowing border to harness.		 中文:
不同的是，這片「兩河之間的土地」（「美索不達米亞」名稱起源的意思）不僅要利用一條水道的洪水，還要利用整個流動邊界的洪水。
English:
However, like ancient Egypt and Kush, its location made it an ideal site for irrigation practices: as the people of the Nile did, Mesopotamians coaxed water into typically drier regions with canals.		 中文:
然而，就像古埃及和庫施一樣，其地理位置使其成為灌溉實踐的理想場所：就像尼羅河沿岸的人們一樣，美索不達米亞人利用運河將水引入通常較乾旱的地區。
English:
The land was routinely fertilized by rich silt washed up by the rivers; Mesopotamia supported the harvest of barley, onions, grapes, apples, and turnips.		 中文:
這片土地常規地被河流沖刷上來的肥沃淤泥施肥；美索不達米亞支持大麥、洋蔥、葡萄、蘋果和蕪菁的收成。
English:
Cattle and sheep grazed on fertile grassland, and fishermen made a living selling and trading their catch.		 中文:
牛羊在肥沃的草地上吃草，漁民以出售和交易他們的漁獲為生。
English:
Like the famous cities of Thebes, Meroe, and Napata, Mesopotamian cities such as Ur and Babylon sat near the rivers, again seeing success on the water because of the agricultural and trade possibilities making commerce possible and merchants rich.		 中文:
就像著名的底比斯、麥羅埃和納帕塔等城市一樣，烏爾和巴比倫等美索不達米亞城市也坐落在河流附近，因為農業和貿易的可能性使商業成為可能並使商人致富，它們在水上再次取得了成功。
English:
In each city, and in each kingdom, success and power were facilitated by both agricultural advancements (such as iron tools and irrigation) and the means to trade and sell a harvest (the rivers).		 中文:
在每個城市和每個王國中，農業的進步（例如鐵器和灌溉）以及貿易和銷售收成的手段（河流）都促進了成功和權力。
English:
The reigns of ancient Egypt, Mesopotamia, and Kush were all dependent on an uncomplicated but involved cycle: the rivers provided the water needed to grow crops like wheat, technology made irrigation, plowing, and harvest possible, and trade generated income to the cities along the river. 		中文:
古埃及、美索不達米亞和庫施的統治都依賴於一個簡單但複雜的循環：河流提供了種植小麥等農作物所需的水，技術使灌溉、犁地和收穫成為可能，而貿易為沿河城市帶來了收入。
English:
In this way, waterfront settlements in each region became seats of commerce and power: they were self-sustaining metropolises. 		中文:
透過這種方式，每個地區的濱水定居點都成為了商業和權力的中心：它們是自我維持的大都會。
English:
The downfall of these cities was also woven with the success and failure of the agriculture of ancient Egypt, Mesopotamia, and Kush.		 中文:
這些城市的衰落也與古埃及、美索不達米亞和庫施農業的成敗交織在一起。
English:
Generations of tough farming and overgrazing sapped land of nutrients, and in Kush, the deforestation that accompanied the mining of iron ore caused devastating erosion.		 中文:
世世代代的艱苦農耕和過度放牧耗盡了土地的養分，而在庫施，伴隨鐵礦開採而來的森林砍伐造成了毀滅性的侵蝕。
English:
As land morphed from oasis to desert, cities lost their power: there was nothing left to grow, and nothing left to trade.		 中文:
隨著土地從綠洲變成沙漠，城市失去了權力：沒有什麼可種植的，也沒有什麼可交易的了。
English:
In spite of their tremendous advances in agricultural technology, these ancient cultures could not combat dust for long, and when farms dried out, so too did the power and success of ancient Egyptian, Kushian, and Mesopotamian cities.		 中文:
儘管他們在農業技術上取得了巨大的進步，但這些古老的文化無法長期對抗沙塵，當農場乾涸時，古埃及、庫施和美索不達米亞城市的權力和成功也隨之消亡。

✏️ 文法重點解析

revolve around (圍繞...旋轉；以...為中心)

表示某事物的核心或主要焦點是另一事物。

Inventions revolving around the rainy season included the system of dikes.
His whole life revolves around his career.
in spite of (儘管)

用於表達雖然有某種情況或條件，但結果卻不受其影響，用法同 despite。

In spite of their tremendous advances, these cultures could not combat dust for long.
We went out in spite of the rain.

💬 重要句型分析

as + S + V, so too did... (隨著...，...也一樣)

用來表示兩件事物同時發生相同的變化或結果。so too did 是倒裝句型，強調後者也發生了與前者相同的事情。

As land morphed from oasis to desert... when farms dried out, so too did the power and success of ancient Egyptian, Kushian, and Mesopotamian cities.

理解測驗
  1. 古埃及、庫施和美索不達米亞農業成功的一個主要原因是什麼？
    • A) 他們有很多黃金可以買食物。
    • B) 他們發展了灌溉和運河等技術來管理河水。
    • C) 他們所有的食物都從其他地區進口。
    • D) 他們只在旱季種植農作物。

    正確答案：B

  2. 鐵的到來對庫施王國有何影響？
    • A) 這導致他們立即失去所有權力。
    • B) 他們完全停止了貿易。
    • C) 這讓他們能夠製造更好的工具，如鋤頭和犁，從而增加農作物產量。
    • D) 他們用鐵建造更堅固的船隻。

    正確答案：C

  3. 最終是什麼導致了這些古城的衰落？
    • A) 他們被外星人入侵。
    • B) 他們停止建造運河。
    • C) 過度放牧和森林砍伐耗盡了養分並造成了侵蝕，將土地變成了沙漠。
    • D) 河流永遠完全停止流動。

    正確答案：C
