✏️ 文法重點解析

it’s hard to + V（很難去...）

使用虛主詞 it 來代替後面真正的主詞 (to + V)，表示做某件事情是困難的。

Now computers are part of the daily life... and it’s hard to imagine a world without them.
It's hard to believe that he actually won the game.
be tied to（與...密切相關，與...綁在一起）

表示兩件事情有很深的連結或關聯性。

The history of Silicon Valley is tied to the history of the semiconductor.
Her success is closely tied to her hard work.

💬 重要句型分析

as long as + S + V（只要...）

引導條件子句，表示在某個前提或條件下，另一件事就會發生或成立。

An engineer could rise to the top quickly, as long as he or she had a good idea.
You can stay here as long as you keep quiet.
not just A, but also B（不僅是A，也是B / 還有B）

這是一個強調兩者的句型，原文使用了變形 "But it isn’t just... it’s also..." 來表達相同的概念。

But it isn’t just the money that draws workers to Silicon Valley—it’s also the opportunity.
He is not just my colleague, but also my best friend.

    • D) Fairchild Semiconductor

    正確答案：D

    • A) 因為這裡是一座山谷
    • B) 因為當地盛產「矽」這項農產品
    • C) 以半導體的骨幹礦物「矽」來命名

    正確答案：C
