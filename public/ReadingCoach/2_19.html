幫助駕駛安全看路

你有沒有坐在車裡遇到下雨？雨、雪和雨夾雪會讓人很難看清楚。在看不清楚的情況下開車是很危險的。很久以前，Mary Anderson 注意到駕駛在惡劣天氣中很難看清楚。雨和雪覆蓋了擋風玻璃。駕駛看不到路。Mary Anderson 發明了一種可以清除擋風玻璃上雨雪的裝置。這就是第一個雨刷！駕駛可以用車內的把手來回移動雨刷擦拭擋風玻璃。多虧了 Mary Anderson，駕駛現在即使在雨中也能安全地看路。今天每輛車都有雨刷！

逐句對照
English:
Have you ever been in a car when it was raining? Rain, snow, and sleet can make it hard to see. It can be dangerous to drive without seeing clearly.		 中文:
你有沒有坐在車裡遇到下雨？雨、雪和雨夾雪會讓人很難看清楚。在看不清楚的情況下開車是很危險的。
Long ago, Mary Anderson noticed that drivers had trouble seeing in bad weather. Rain and snow covered the windshield. Drivers could not see the road. 很久以前，Mary Anderson 注意到駕駛在惡劣天氣中很難看清楚。雨和雪覆蓋了擋風玻璃。駕駛看不到路。
Mary Anderson invented a device that could clear rain and snow from a windshield. This was the first windshield wiper! The driver could use a handle inside the car to move the wiper back and forth across the windshield. Mary Anderson 發明了一種可以清除擋風玻璃上雨雪的裝置。這就是第一個雨刷！駕駛可以用車內的把手來回移動雨刷擦拭擋風玻璃。
Thanks to Mary Anderson, drivers can now see safely even in the rain. Every car today has windshield wipers! 多虧了 Mary Anderson，駕駛現在即使在雨中也能安全地看路。今天每輛車都有雨刷！

✏️ 文法重點解析

1. 過去式敘述歷史發明
Mary Anderson noticed that drivers had trouble seeing.
She invented the first windshield wiper.
2. "Without..." 沒有...
Without wipers, drivers could not see the road clearly.
3. "Thanks to..." 多虧了...
Thanks to Mary Anderson, drivers can see safely in the rain.

💬 重要句型分析

句型 1: noticed + that 子句
Mary noticed that drivers had trouble seeing in the rain.
句型 2: invented + a device + that 子句
She invented a device that could clear rain from a windshield.
句型 3: It + was + too + 形容詞 + to + V
It was too dangerous to drive without seeing clearly.
💡 "Invent" 詞彙家族
  • invent (動詞) → 發明
  • inventor (名詞) → 發明者
  • invention (名詞) → 發明物
