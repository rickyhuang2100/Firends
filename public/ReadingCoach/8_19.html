Legend has it that two Chinese papermakers were captured during the battle and brought to Samarkand, where they were ordered to establish a paper manufacturing center in the heart of the Muslim empire.		 中文:
傳說有兩名中國造紙工匠在戰鬥中被俘，並被帶到撒馬爾罕，在那裡他們被命令在穆斯林帝國的中心建立一個造紙中心。
English:
Whether or not this was wholly true, it is probable that Muslim warriors encountered papermaking during their battles with China.		 中文:
無論這是否完全真實，穆斯林戰士在與中國的戰鬥中很可能接觸到了造紙術。
English:
Seeing its usefulness immediately, they returned home with the technology to share with their scribes.		 中文:
他們立即看到了它的用處，帶著這項技術回到家鄉與他們的抄寫員分享。
English:
The Muslim empire was a culture that placed great importance on words.		 中文:
穆斯林帝國是一個非常重視文字的文化。
English:
The Muslims’ holy book, the Qur’an, was a great poetic work, and devout Muslims were expected to memorize parts of it to be recited during religious ceremonies.		 中文:
穆斯林的聖書《古蘭經》是一部偉大的詩歌作品，虔誠的穆斯林被期望記住其中的一部分，以便在宗教儀式中背誦。
English:
One of their most famous art forms was calligraphy, a style of decorative writing, and their great mosques were decorated not with images of people, but the words of the Qur’an itself.		 中文:
他們最著名的藝術形式之一是書法，一種裝飾性的寫作風格，他們偉大的清真寺不是用人物圖像裝飾的，而是用《古蘭經》本身的文字。
English:
It is no surprise that a society which so valued words would fall in love with paper.		 中文:
一個如此重視文字的社會會愛上紙張，這並不奇怪。
English:
Compared to parchment and papyrus, even rudimentary paper—much rougher and thicker than the pages of a book today—was a great improvement.		 中文:
與羊皮紙和莎草紙相比，即使是粗糙的紙張——比今天書本的書頁粗糙和厚得多——也是一個巨大的進步。
English:
Unlike parchment, which could only be made from the skins of animals, paper could be manufactured relatively cheaply and easily.		 中文:
與只能用動物皮製成的羊皮紙不同，紙張的製造相對便宜和容易。
English:
Unlike papyrus, paper did not crack when it was bent.		 中文:
與莎草紙不同，紙張彎曲時不會破裂。
English:
It was also thinner, which made the binding of books much simpler than it had been before.		 中文:
它也更薄，這使得書籍的裝訂比以前簡單得多。
English:
Perhaps most importantly, paper absorbed ink, which meant that anything written on paper could not be erased.		 中文:
也許最重要的是，紙張會吸收墨水，這意味著寫在紙上的任何東西都無法被擦除。
English:
That's not very useful when you're trying to solve a math problem, but when writing official government documents, permanence is crucial.		 中文:
當你試圖解決數學問題時這不是很有用，但是當撰寫官方政府文件時，持久性（不可更改性）是至關重要的。
English:
Just as it had been in China, paper was originally used mainly by the Arab government.		 中文:
就像在中國一樣，紙張最初主要被阿拉伯政府使用。
English:
At a time when Europe was broken into a number of disorganized kingdoms, the Abbasid Caliphate, which stretched from Afghanistan to North Africa, might have been the greatest power on Earth.		 中文:
在歐洲分裂為許多雜亂無章的王國的時期，從阿富汗延伸到北非的阿拔斯哈里發國（阿拔斯王朝），可能是地球上最大的強權。
English:
This sprawling empire contained dozens of great cities, millions of people, and several different, often conflicting, cultures.		 中文:
這個龐大的帝國包含了幾十個偉大的城市、數百萬人口，以及幾種不同、常常相互衝突的文化。
English:
Although the empire's official language was Arabic, its people spoke many different languages—from Persian in the East to Berber in the West.		 中文:
雖然帝國的官方語言是阿拉伯語，但其人民說著許多不同的語言——從東方的波斯語到西方的柏柏爾語。
English:
Now those disparate regions could be united by the written word.		 中文:
現在那些不同的地區可以透過文字統一起來了。
English:
Paper's use spread quickly.		 中文:
紙張的使用迅速傳播開來。
English:
By the end of the 8th century, a paper mill had been set up in Baghdad.		 中文:
到8世紀末，巴格達已經建立了一家造紙廠。
English:
Over the next 200 years, the technology spread to Syria, North Africa, and even Spain—which was partially ruled by Muslims from 711 to 1492.		 中文:
在接下來的200年裡，這項技術傳播到了敘利亞、北非，甚至西班牙——從711年到1492年，西班牙部分地區由穆斯林統治。
English:
Although the Abbasid Caliphate and other ruling houses of the Arab empire were skilled in war, they prized learning above all else.		 中文:
雖然阿拔斯哈里發國和阿拉伯帝國的其他統治家族擅長戰爭，但他們最重視學習。
English:
The Abbasids took inspiration from a well-known saying, "The ink of a scholar is holier than the blood of a martyr."		 中文:
阿拔斯人從一句名言中獲得靈感：「學者的墨水比烈士的鮮血更神聖。」
English:
To this end, the Abbasids did everything they could to encourage scientific achievement.		 中文:
為此，阿拔斯人盡一切可能鼓勵科學成就。
English:
As Europe languished in the Dark Ages, when even literacy was limited mainly to monks, the Muslim world entered a scientific golden age.		 中文:
當歐洲在黑暗時代苦苦掙扎、識字率主要局限於僧侶時，穆斯林世界進入了科學的黃金時代。
English:
Building on the knowledge of the ancient Greeks, Muslim scholars made great advances in geometry, astronomy, and mathematics.		 中文:
在古希臘人知識的基礎上，穆斯林學者在幾何學、天文學和數學方面取得了巨大進步。
English:
As paper became more widespread, so did knowledge.		 中文:
隨著紙張變得越來越普及，知識也隨之普及。

透過採用裝配線式的技術，手工寫書的繁瑣過程變得更容易了，這使得一群抄寫員能比以往更快地生產出成品書籍。
English:
Great libraries were established across the Muslim world, and in capitals like Baghdad and Cairo, booksellers flourished.		 中文:
偉大的圖書館在整個穆斯林世界建立起來，在巴格達和開羅等首都，書商蓬勃發展。

Scholars and poets were supported by the government, and for perhaps the first time in history, it became possible to make a living by reading and writing alone.		 中文:
學者和詩人受到政府的支持，這也許是歷史上第一次，單靠閱讀和寫作就能謀生。

儘管紙張變得普及，但在一個地方它的主導地位受到了抵制：宗教。

Because paper was less expensive than parchment, it was initially considered unfit to bear the holy words of the Qur’an.		 中文:
因為紙比羊皮紙便宜，最初它被認為不適合用來承載《古蘭經》的神聖文字。

已知最古老的寫在紙上的《古蘭經》可追溯到971-972年。

The western spread of paper didn’t end in the Arab empire.		 中文:
紙張向西傳播並沒有在阿拉伯帝國結束。
English:
From there, the technology to manufacture paper made its way through Europe.		 中文:
從那裡，製造紙張的技術傳遍了整個歐洲。

It was not until the middle of the 15th century that a German blacksmith named Johannes Gutenberg invented a printing press that allowed for mass production of the Christian Bible.		 中文:
直到15世紀中葉，一位名叫約翰尼斯·谷登堡 (Johannes Gutenberg) 的德國鐵匠發明了印刷機，這使得基督教聖經的大規模生產成為可能。
English:
Most of Gutenberg's Bibles were printed on paper, becoming one of the most famous early works on paper in history.		 中文:
谷登堡的大多數聖經都是印在紙上的，成為歷史上最著名的早期紙本作品之一。

✏️ 文法重點解析

Legend has it that... (傳說...)

用來引述一個沒有確鑿證據的古老故事或傳說。

Legend has it that two Chinese papermakers were captured.
Legend has it that a ghost haunts this castle.
make a living (謀生)

指賺取足夠的錢來支付生活必需品。

It became possible to make a living by reading and writing alone.
He makes a living as a freelance writer.

💬 重要句型分析

It is no surprise that... (...並不奇怪)

用來表示某件事是非常合乎邏輯或可以預期的。

It is no surprise that a society which so valued words would fall in love with paper.

理解測驗
  1. 為什麼紙張比羊皮紙和莎草紙進步？
    • A) 它的製作難度更高。
    • B) 彎曲時容易破裂。
    • C) 它比較便宜，彎曲時不會破裂，而且能永久吸收墨水。
    • D) 它更重、更厚。

    正確答案：C

  2. 阿拔斯王朝如何看待學習和科學？
    • A) 他們試圖阻止人們閱讀。
    • B) 他們最重視學習並支持學者。
    • C) 他們認為只有戰爭才是重要的。
    • D) 他們認為科學是危險的。

    正確答案：B

  3. 為什麼紙張最初在穆斯林帝國被抵制用於宗教？
    • A) 在上面寫字太難了。
    • B) 大家都認為這太貴了。
    • C) 因為它比羊皮紙便宜，被認為不適合承載神聖的文字。
    • D) 因為它是在中國發明的。

    正確答案：C
