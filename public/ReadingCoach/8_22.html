逐句對照
English:
Do you know where the most expensive house in the universe is located?		 中文:
你知道宇宙中最昂貴的房子在哪裡嗎？
English:
Some might guess Hollywood, where some of the richest and most famous movie stars have their homes.		 中文:
有些人可能會猜好萊塢，那裡是一些最富有、最著名的電影明星的家。
English:
Others might think of New York City, where a one-bedroom apartment in Manhattan can cost more than a mansion in the suburbs.		 中文:
其他人可能會想到紐約市，在那裡曼哈頓的一間單臥室公寓的價格可能比郊區的豪宅還要高。
English:
But they would all be wrong, because this is a trick question.		 中文:
但他們都錯了，因為這是一個陷阱題（腦筋急轉彎）。
English:
The most expensive house isn’t even properly located on Earth.		 中文:
最昂貴的房子甚至不完全位在地球上。
English:
It’s the International Space Station (ISS), which is circling in orbit above us right now.		 中文:
它是國際太空站 (ISS)，目前正 在我們上方的軌道上運行。
English:
The cost to build this engineering marvel, which is roughly the size of a football field, is around 150 billion dollars.		 中文:
建造這個大約有足球場那麼大的工程奇蹟，成本約為 1500 億美元。
English:
Many different governments cooperated in order to plan and build the ISS, including the USA, Russia, Japan, Canada, and Europe.		 中文:
許多不同的政府為了計畫和建造國際太空站而合作，包括美國、俄羅斯、日本、加拿大和歐洲。
English:
These entities decided to work together on the project only after developing plans independently for related space projects.		 中文:
這些實體是在各自獨立制定相關太空計畫之後，才決定共同合作這個計畫。
English:
By combining forces, they reasoned, they could split the cost of constructing a space station and also share resources while onboard the station.		 中文:
他們推論，透過結合力量，他們可以分攤建造太空站的成本，並且在太空站上也能共享資源。
English:
The countries envisioned three important purposes for their joint project: to support scientific research, to help astronauts continue to explore space, and to educate the public.		 中文:
這些國家為他們的聯合計畫設想了三個重要目的：支持科學研究、幫助太空人繼續探索太空，以及教育大眾。
English:
Thus, the engineering criteria for the space station had to include facilities to support each of these important missions.		 中文:
因此，太空站的工程標準必須包括支援這些重要任務中每一項的設施。
English:
Construction began in 1998, after the countries decided to band together and merge their space missions to create the ISS.		 中文:
建設始於1998年，在這些國家決定團結起來合併他們的太空任務以創建國際太空站之後。
English:
Many countries used their spacecraft to deliver the parts for the ISS, little by little, into space.		 中文:
許多國家使用他們的太空船一點一點地將國際太空站的零件運送到太空中。
English:
First came the operating systems and hardware.		 中文:
首先是作業系統和硬體。
English:
Then, two years later, a Russian rocket delivered the living quarters (complete with beds, toilets, and a kitchen) that would make the ISS habitable for humans.		 中文:
然後，兩年後，一枚俄羅斯火箭運送了居住區（配有床、廁所和廚房），這使得國際太空站適合人類居住。
English:
The first “residents” of the ISS—two Russian astronauts and one American—arrived on Expedition 1 in 2000.		 中文:
國際太空站的第一批「居民」——兩名俄羅斯太空人和一名美國太空人——於2000年搭乘「遠征1號」抵達。
English:
Over time, more space missions to the ISS added new parts to the space station, such as “docks” for incoming spacecraft that would make it easier for astronauts to come in and out of the station.		 中文:
隨著時間的推移，越來越多前往國際太空站的太空任務為太空站增加了新零件，例如為來訪的太空船設置的「停靠站(docks)」，這使得太空人進出太空站變得更加容易。
English:
Throughout the construction of the ISS, which is partially solar-powered, engineers had to think constantly about the best way to keep the ISS running.		 中文:
在部分由太陽能供電的國際太空站的整個建設過程中，工程師們必須不斷思考保持國際太空站運作的最佳方式。
English:
They had to build and position the station’s parts so that the space station could be powered by light from the sun.		 中文:
他們必須建造和定位太空站的零件，以便太空站可以由太陽光供電。
English:
They also had to think about ways to protect it from meteoroids (including installing strong shutters on its seven windows).		 中文:
他們還必須考慮保護它免受流星體侵害的方法（包括在其七個窗戶上安裝堅固的百葉窗）。
English:
They installed robotic “arms” for the space station that could grab and hold both ships and astronauts securely while docking.		 中文:
他們為太空站安裝了機器「手臂」，可以在對接時安全地抓住和固定船隻和太空人。
English:
And they had to install features that would make it easier to live for long stretches of time in space, such as exercise machines for the astronauts.		 中文:
而且他們必須安裝一些設施，讓在太空中長時間生活變得更容易，例如供太空人使用的運動器材。
English:
Astronauts can come and go on the ISS.		 中文:
太空人可以在國際太空站上來來去去。
English:
They come to perform many of the experiments for which the station was designed, involving biology, physics, astronomy, and meteorology.		 中文:
他們來進行許多太空站設計用來執行的實驗，涉及生物學、物理學、天文學和氣象學。
English:
Others test equipment to be used in missions to the moon and Mars.		 中文:
其他人則測試將用於前往月球和火星任務的設備。
English:
In a Japanese-built laboratory aboard the ISS called Kibo (which means “hope”), they can even grow plants and raise fish.		 中文:
在國際太空站上一個名為希望號（Kibo，意為「希望」）的日本建造實驗室中，他們甚至可以種植植物和養魚。
English:
However, most of the astronauts’ space food is still delivered in sealed bags, and there isn’t much variety.		 中文:
然而，大多數太空人的太空食品仍然以密封袋運送，而且種類不多。
English:
Thus, the crew aboard the ISS often looks forward to visiting shuttles that bring the astronauts fresh, different fruit to eat.		 中文:
因此，國際太空站上的機組人員經常期待來訪的太空梭為太空人帶來新鮮、不同的水果來吃。
English:
Life aboard the ISS has become relatively more comfortable thanks to technological improvements developed by engineers; however, it has not always been easy for the engineers back home to work on the space station.		 中文:
由於工程師開發的技術改進，國際太空站上的生活已經變得相對更舒適；然而，對國內的工程師來說，在太空站上工作並不總是那麼容易。
English:
Space travel and construction of spacecraft are two of the most expensive projects a country can take on, and as the economies around the world shift, some countries have a harder time contributing financially.		 中文:
太空旅行和太空船的建造是一個國家可以承擔的最昂貴的項目之二，隨著全球經濟的轉變，一些國家在提供財政貢獻方面遇到了更大的困難。
English:
Sometimes, engineers from different countries will disagree about the best way to build something.		 中文:
有時，來自不同國家的工程師會對建造某物的最佳方式產生分歧。
English:
And while some people on the space station project think it’s a good idea to charge money to space “tourists” in order to provide more funds for the project or to charge companies a lot of money to advertise their business on the rockets that fly to the ISS, others think that these ideas do not align with the original purposes of the ISS.		 中文:
雖然太空站專案上的一些人認為向太空「遊客」收費以提供更多專案資金，或向公司收取大筆費用以在飛往國際太空站的火箭上做廣告是個好主意，但其他人認為這些想法與國際太空站的初衷不符。
English:
But the fact is, no country or individual can afford the giant price tag for this important space “house” alone, so they must keep working together.		 中文:
但事實是，沒有任何一個國家或個人能獨自負擔這座重要太空「房屋」的巨大價格標籤，所以他們必須繼續合作。
English:
And the results—whether they include important new scientific discoveries, easier and more frequent missions to Mars, or better cultural relations between our countries—are sure to benefit us.		 中文:
而結果——無論它們是否包括重要的新科學發現、更容易和更頻繁的火星任務，或是我們各國之間更好的文化關係——肯定會使我們受益。

✏️ 文法重點解析

band together (團結起來)

表示為了共同的目的或目標而聯合起來。

The countries decided to band together and merge their space missions.
The neighbors banded together to clean up the park.
little by little (一點一點地；逐漸地)

表示某事是逐漸地或分批發生的。

Many countries used their spacecraft to deliver the parts, little by little.
Little by little, she learned to speak French.

💬 重要句型分析

whether A, B, or C... (無論是 A, B 還是 C...)

用來列舉多種可能的情況，並表示在這些情況下結果都是一樣的。

Whether they include important new scientific discoveries, easier and more frequent missions to Mars, or better cultural relations between our countries—are sure to benefit us.

🎯 閱讀理解

理解測驗
  1. 根據文章，宇宙中最昂貴的房子是什麼？
    • A) 好萊塢的豪宅。
    • B) 紐約市的公寓。
    • C) 國際太空站 (ISS)。
    • D) 歐洲的城堡。

    正確答案：C

  2. 為什麼多個國家決定合作建立國際太空站？
    • A) 因為一個國家沒有足夠的空間來建造它。
    • B) 為了分攤成本和共享資源。
    • C) 因為他們被聯合國強迫。
    • D) 為了在太空競賽中競爭。

    正確答案：B

  3. 在太空站工作提到的挑戰之一是什麼？
    • A) 在太空中種植植物是不可能的。
    • B) 太空人不想去國際太空站。
    • C) 一些國家在財政貢獻上有困難，工程師有時在如何建造東西上存在分歧。
    • D) 國際太空站上的窗戶太多了。

    正確答案：C
