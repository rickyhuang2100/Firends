📖 重點單字與片語

furiously
adv. 副詞
猛烈地；狂怒地
She is furiously slamming her fists at a punching bag.
ferocious
adj. 形容詞
兇猛的；殘暴的
Her coach calls Kennedy “ferocious.”
epidemic
n. 名詞
流行病；（壞事）蔓延
There’s an epidemic of violence against women.
harrowing
adj. 形容詞
令人痛心的；悲慘的
The department released a harrowing statistic.
indigenous
adj. 形容詞
原住民的；本土的
More than 100 indigenous women have gone missing.
perpetrator
n. 名詞
犯罪者；行兇者
Perpetrators of violence are rarely held accountable.
accountable
adj. 形容詞
應負責任的
They are rarely held accountable by law enforcement.
fend
v. 動詞
照料；防禦
The girls are left to fend for themselves.
haven
n. 名詞
避風港；避難所
It was serving as a safe haven for the community’s girls.

✏️ 文法重點解析

get away with (逃脫懲罰)

表示做錯事或犯罪後沒有受到應有的懲罰。

Because the attackers so often get away with these crimes, the girls are left to fend for themselves.
He thought he could lie and get away with it.
in awe of (對...感到敬畏/驚嘆)

表示對某人或某事感到驚奇、敬畏或欽佩。

Others have gathered to watch, in awe of Kennedy’s speed and skill.
We were all in awe of the beautiful scenery.

💬 重要句型分析

Prior to + V-ing/N (在...之前)

等同於 before，但語氣較正式，後面接動名詞或名詞。

Prior to taking up boxing, she was quiet and shy.

🎯 閱讀理解

理解測驗
  1. 法蘭克·奇普 (Frank Kipp) 最初成立黑腳原住民族拳擊俱樂部的目的是什麼？
    • A) 訓練女孩成為奧運拳擊手。
    • B) 幫助孩子們遠離毒品和酒精。
    • C) 為ESPN製作一部紀錄片。
    • D) 在保留區成立一支警察部隊。

    正確答案：B

  2. MMIW 代表什麼？
    • A) 華盛頓的許多男人 (Many Men In Washington)
    • B) 失蹤和被謀殺的原住民婦女 (Missing and Murdered Indigenous Women)
    • C) 蒙大拿州最重要的女人 (Montana's Most Important Women)
    • D) 我媽媽在看著 (My Mother Is Watching)

    正確答案：B

  3. 根據米絲蒂·拉普蘭特 (Misty LaPlant) 的說法，對原住民婦女施暴的加害者通常會怎樣？
    • A) 他們總是被抓住並關進監獄。
    • B) 他們被迫學習打拳擊。
    • C) 他們很少被執法部門追究責任。
    • D) 他們必須向受害者家屬道歉。

    正確答案：C
