✏️ 文法重點解析

It was not until... that... (直到...才...)

強調句型，用來強調直到某個時間點或事件發生之後，另一件事情才開始發生。

It was not until attending graduate school that Chimamanda majored in creative writing.
It was not until yesterday that I realized the truth.
Despite + N/V-ing (儘管...)

介系詞，表示雖然有某種情況，但結果卻出乎意料，後面接名詞或動名詞。

Despite her early passion for literature, she did not initially want to be a writer.
Despite raining heavily, they went out for a walk.

💬 重要句型分析

focus on... (專注於...；以...為重點)

表示將注意力或重點放在某個人事物上。

One of her more recent novels focuses on a Nigerian girl's experience.
You should focus on your studies.
part of the year (一年中的一部分時間)

用來描述某段不特定的時間長度，與另一段時間相對應。

She lives part of the year in Nigeria and part of the year in the United States.
