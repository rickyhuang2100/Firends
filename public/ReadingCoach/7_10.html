The typical depiction of nature in Islamic art is abstract.		 中文:
伊斯蘭藝術中對自然的典型描繪是抽象的。
English:
This approach departs from Europe’s naturalistic approach, which traditionally aimed to portray nature in a realistic way. 		中文:
這種方法有別於歐洲的自然主義方法，後者傳統上旨在以寫實的方式描繪自然。
English:
Plants and flowers looked exactly like real plants and flowers in European art, while in Islamic art, these natural objects would often be more geometric and linear.		 中文:
在歐洲藝術中，植物和花卉看起來完全像真實的植物和花卉，而在伊斯蘭藝術中，這些自然物體通常更具幾何形狀和線條感。
English:
Islamic art is known for being geometric, so geometric vegetal art fit into the prominent aesthetic.		 中文:
伊斯蘭藝術以幾何圖形著稱，因此幾何植物藝術融入了這種顯著的審美觀中。
English:
Vegetal patterns can be found everywhere in Islamic art and often serve an ornamental function.		 中文:
植物圖案在伊斯蘭藝術中無處不在，且通常具有裝飾功能。
English:
One of the reasons for their popular usage is that some sects of Islam forbid artists from representing the natural world in any of its living forms.		 中文:
它們被廣泛使用的原因之一是，伊斯蘭教的一些教派禁止藝術家以任何生命形式來表現自然界。
English:
Consequently, artists were not allowed to represent plants or flowers in their artwork.		 中文:
因此，藝術家不被允許在他們的藝術作品中描繪植物或花卉。
English:
Another reason can be attributed to their origin.		 中文:
另一個原因可以歸因於它們的起源。
English:
Art historians believe that the rulers of the early Islamic empire encouraged artists to focus on creating impressive quantities of high-scale objects.		 中文:
藝術史學家認為，早期伊斯蘭帝國的統治者鼓勵藝術家專注於創作大量令人印象深刻的大型物品。
English:
As a result, artists did not focus their energy on inventing new designs, and instead relied on the artistic practices of Rome, Egypt, and the Byzantine empire.		 中文:
結果，藝術家沒有把精力集中在發明新設計上，而是依賴羅馬、埃及和拜占庭帝國的藝術實踐。
English:
Over time, however, Islamic artists departed from these established artistic traditions and Islamic vegetal patterns became highly stylized and geometric.		 中文:

✏️ 文法重點解析

forbid + sb + from + V-ing (禁止某人做某事)

表示不允許某人進行某個動作。

Some sects of Islam forbid artists from representing the natural world.
The school forbids students from bringing their phones to class.
while (然而；而)

連接詞，用來引導兩個對比或相反的情況。

Plants and flowers looked exactly like real plants in European art, while in Islamic art, these natural objects would often be more geometric.
I like coffee, while my brother prefers tea.

💬 重要句型分析

be known for (以...聞名)

表示因為某種特色或事物而廣為人知。

Islamic art is known for being geometric.
Paris is known for its beautiful architecture.
attribute... to... / be attributed to (將...歸因於...)

表示認為某事物的發生是因為另一個原因。

Another reason can be attributed to their origin.
She attributes her success to hard work.

🎯 閱讀理解

理解測驗
  1. 伊斯蘭藝術通常如何描繪自然？
    • A) 以高度寫實的方式
    • B) 透過動物的詳細畫作
    • C) 以抽象和幾何的方式
    • D) 完全像真實的植物和花朵

    正確答案：C

  2. 根據文章，為什麼伊斯蘭藝術家有時被禁止描繪生命形式？
    • A) 因為他們缺乏技巧
    • B) 因為伊斯蘭教的一些教派禁止這樣做
    • C) 因為他們找不到任何植物
    • D) 因為他們更喜歡畫建築物

    正確答案：B

  3. 當早期統治者要求伊斯蘭藝術家快速創作大量藝術品時，他們依賴什麼？
    • A) 新的原創設計
    • B) 羅馬、埃及和拜占庭帝國的藝術實踐
    • C) 歐洲的自然主義方法
    • D) 現代幾何軟體

    正確答案：B
