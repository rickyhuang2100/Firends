✏️ 文法重點解析

Even though (儘管；雖然)

連接詞，用來引導讓步狀語子句，表示「雖然...但是...」的意思，語氣比 although 更強烈。

Even though her career flourished, many other actors have not had the same opportunities.
Even though it was raining, we still went for a walk.
no matter... (無論...)

用來表示不管什麼情況，結果都不會改變，後面常接疑問詞如 what, who, how 等，或者直接接名詞。

...pursue their dreams no matter the obstacle.
No matter what happens, I will always support you.

💬 重要句型分析

dedicate... to... (把...奉獻給...；致力於...)

表示將時間、精力或生命完全投入到某件事情上，to 為介系詞，後面接名詞或動名詞。

Salonga dedicated her life to performing.
He dedicated his time to helping others.
blow away (使...大為驚訝 / 讚嘆)

口語用法，表示某事物非常出色，讓人感到極度驚訝或佩服。

They were blown away by her powerful voice.
The magic trick completely blew me away.
