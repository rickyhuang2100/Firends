場景一：Phoebe 的「鼓」表演

Phoebe 用大肚子當鼓伴奏，唱起《Smelly Cat》，展現她的幽默與自信。


Phoebe: "It is true what they say: Pregnant bellies look like a drum... I'm so pregnant that my guitar doesn't fit anymore. So I'm going to play my songs on this drum. It sounds really cool! Listen, listen. Smelly cat, smelly cat. What are they feeding you?"

菲比把懷孕肚子說成鼓，並用《Smelly Cat》來逗笑大家。這段話很適合 AB Loop，可以幫你練習說話節奏與口語韻律。

場景二：幫寶寶取名

Phoebe 和 Frank 討論三胞胎名字，對話中有很多幽默與誇張的命名方式。


Phoebe: "I want to name the girl baby Leslie. And Frank wants to name one of the boy babies... Frank Jr., Jr. Wouldn't that be Frank III? Don't get me started. Anyway, since there are three babies... and we both got to put our names in... we would be truly honored if you would name the other boy baby."

這段對話有許多口語轉折句和幽默用法，特別適合用 AB Loop 重複聽出節奏與句子結構。

場景三：討論寶寶名字的理由

大家輪流幫寶寶取名，從時尚、幽默到「The Hulk」，句子充滿強烈個性。


Rachel: "Do we have a baby name yet? No. It's so hard! I went through this book and found nothing. I want a name that's really strong and confident. You know? Like... Exxon!"
Phoebe: "You want a strong name? How about The Hulk?"
Rachel: "I like the idea of naming him after someone I love. Joey and Chandler are both great names. But with Joey, if you need him, he'll be there. Chandler will be there too. He might be a little late... but he'll be there. And he'll bring you cold soda... if what you need him for is that you're really hot. What do you say?"

這段最適合練習「比較句」與「條件句」，也能觀察口語中 how about、what do you say、if you need him 的用法。

III. 詞彙

look like a drum 片語 看起來像鼓 "Pregnant bellies look like a drum." 表示肚子圓鼓鼓，很適合形容懷孕時的肚型。
name the girl baby 片語 幫女嬰取名 "I want to name the girl baby Leslie." 是英語中常用的命名句型。也可改成 "name the baby"。
Don't get me started 口語片語 別讓我開始說/說了我就生氣 "Don't get me started." 用於對方提到敏感話題時，表示你一說下去就會滔滔不絕或生氣。

How about... 詢問句型 ...怎麼樣？ "How about The Hulk?" 表示提出建議或選項。

IV. 語法與發音

📌 語法焦點："If you need him, he'll be there"

這句話是條件句的常見用法，表示「如果你需要他的時候，他就會在那裡」。

  • If you need him: 第一條件句，用現在式表示未來可能的情況。
  • he'll be there: 未來式表示結果。
  • 整句可用於保證或承諾，例如："If you need me, I'll help you."

📌 口語發音："How about..." 和 "What do you say?"

How about 的發音通常連成一片：/haʊ əˈbaʊt/，中文可先念成「哈伯」+「外特」。

What do you say? 口語裡常略讀成 "What d'ya say?"，聽起來像 /wʌt də ju seɪ/。

這兩句都很常用於提出建議或請求對方意見，是 AB Loop 練習對話時的好句子。
